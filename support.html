Get Stuff Done Randomly — Support

Help and contact for the iPhone and iPad app

Contact

Questions, bug reports or feedback? Email getstuffdoneapp@proton.me. If something isn't working, please tell us your iPhone or iPad model, your iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened. Replies usually take a few days.

Frequently asked questions

How does "Pick something for me" work?

It chooses one task at random from your Today list. For multi-step tasks, only the next unfinished step can be picked, so you always get one small thing to do.

What's the difference between Today and All Tasks?

Today is your short list for right now. All Tasks holds everything else, sorted into Priority, In the next week, and In the next month. Swipe a task right in All Tasks to add it to Today, and swipe left to remove it. Use the six-dot handle on a task to drag it to a different group.

What are Templates?

Templates are reusable groups of tasks, such as a morning routine or a weekly reset. Swipe a template task right to put a fresh copy on Today. Editing or deleting a template never changes copies you've already added.

Where is my data stored? Can I get it back if I delete the app?

Your tasks are stored only on your device. There is no account and no cloud copy, so deleting the app, or resetting your device without restoring a device backup, removes them for good. See the privacy policy for details.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Everything works without an internet connection.

How do I change the theme or turn off the celebration screen?

Open the Settings tab. You can switch between the Vibrant and Neutral themes and turn the celebration screen on or off there.

How do I delete all my data?

Delete the app from your device. Everything it stored is removed with it.
