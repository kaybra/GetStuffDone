Privacy Policy — GSD MCH

Last updated: 19 September 2026

GSD MCH ("the app") is a simple chore-tracking app. This page explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

The app does not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal information on any server. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, and no tracking. Everything you enter — your chores, your progress — stays on your own device.

What data the app stores, and where

The app saves your chore list using your device's local storage, a standard feature built into every web browser and app runtime. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not visible to the app's developer or to anyone else
  • Is not backed up to any cloud service by the app itself
  • Can be permanently deleted at any time by deleting the app, or by clearing the app's data in your device settings

Because nothing is stored anywhere but your own device, if you delete the app or reset your device without backing this data up yourself, it cannot be recovered.

What the app does not do

  • No account creation or login
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No collection of your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information
  • No sharing or selling of data to third parties — there is no data to share

Third-party links and fonts

The app includes a couple of optional outbound links that open outside the app in your device's browser:

  • A link to a YouTube channel (credit for creative inspiration)
  • A link to a Ko-fi page (an optional way to support the developer)

These are operated by YouTube (Google) and Ko-fi respectively, and are each covered by their own privacy policies once you leave the app. The app also loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, which may process standard technical information (such as your IP address) as part of delivering those fonts, under Google's own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that does handle data differently — this page will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.
